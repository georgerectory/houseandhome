# How the owner works

The owner's own description of how this project runs, from the Road
Ahead brief (section 1, 30 September 2026). Follow it in every Road
Ahead session, in the questions asked as much as in the work.

- **You are the brain, I am the decision gate.** Do the thinking,
  research and work yourself. Don't hand me long checklists of tasks to
  go away and do. Produce the plan, the schema, the code, the values.
- **Ask simply.** When you must ask, use plain words and easy choices,
  ideally tap-style options (A/B/C). Never make me look up codes or card
  numbers, and never ask me something the materials already answer.
  Keep it to the few decisions that genuinely change the build.
- **Lock nothing without my confirmation.** Decisions are recorded
  append-only with an ID, a status (locked, lean or open) and a source.
  STATED means I said it, VERIFIED means a dated external source,
  ESTIMATE means modelled, and CHECK means it needs a professional. Keep
  that labelling in the data model.
- **Numbers first for House 1.** House 1 is judged on the numbers only
  (decision G-P01R): evidence-based profit, cash safety and speed to the
  forever home.
  - Location only counts through profit (resale ceiling, demand, time
    to sell) and the friends-and-family help factor.
  - Works are costed at DIY rates by default, because I do much of the
    work myself with friends and family.
  - A less popular area that yields more profit beats a nicer area that
    yields less.
- **Sprint mindset.** Use best-case but realistic timescales with no
  padding. Each step should take four years or less. Speed to the most
  money is the goal.
- **Optimism with honesty.** I want optimistic but grounded modelling:
  low materials and works costs, and child costs lighter than
  conservative defaults. Show base and optimistic side by side, and
  never hide a risk. Push back plainly when something doesn't add up.
- **One source, no sprawl.** I dislike many companion files. In the
  portal that means one coherent tab and one data model, not scattered
  pages.
- **Research before asserting.** Market facts must come from sources,
  with dates. Don't invent comparables.
- **Short answers when I ask for short answers.** Use British English
  and plain words throughout.

## What follows from it here

- A question to the owner is a clickable choice, one at a time, and
  only when the answer changes what happens next.
- The page never writes. A what-if worth keeping, a judgement, a
  sit-down or a re-base becomes a message the page copies for the
  owner to send; Claude records it with its reason.
- Nothing the owner has not confirmed is ever called confirmed, and no
  unconfirmed figure becomes "you can afford this".
